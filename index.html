About the club

Who we are, where we came from.

Galacticoß BPIE was founded in Mexico '70 as a fifth-division side with nothing but a kit and a dream. Five decades on, the club has been everywhere a football club can go — all the way up to the top flight in Italy '90, and back down to Division 3 by South Africa '10 — before clawing its way back to a settled Division 2 outfit heading into the 2030 season.

The club now plays in Málaga blue and white, and the crest carries the same castle-and-sea imagery that's watched over the city's own football club for generations. It's a "Well Known" outfit these days, with a reported 24 million fans across every platform the boys play on.

The current squad runs a 4-4-2: new signing The Joker between the sticks, an AI-manned back four holding the defence, John Barnes and Pacey Lacey providing the width on the flanks, Santi "El Capitano" Cazorla and Emilio "El Loco" Bur controlling the middle, and Zhuge Liang and El Tecla leading the line together up top.
