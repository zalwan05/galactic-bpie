About the club

Who we are, where we came from.

Galacticoß BPIE was founded in Mexico '70 as a fifth-division side with nothing but a kit and a dream. Five decades on, the club has been everywhere a football club can go — all the way up to the top flight in Italy '90, and back down to Division 3 by South Africa '10 — before clawing its way back to a settled Division 2 outfit heading into the 2030 season.

The club now plays in Málaga blue and white, and the crest carries the same castle-and-sea imagery that's watched over the city's own football club for generations. It's a "Well Known" outfit these days, with a reported 24 million fans across every platform the boys play on.

The current squad runs a 4-1-4-1: new signing The Joker between the sticks, El Tecla dropped into an otherwise AI-manned back four, Emilio "El Loco" Bur screening alone in front of the defence, John Barnes and Nicolas Platano providing the width with Santi "El Capitano" Cazorla and Pacey Lacey through the middle, and Zhuge Liang leading the line on his own up top.

Match report · Division 2 · Jul 30, 2026

Galacticos are up: 2–0 over Yaadballers seals Division 1 return

By Marco Villalta, Galacticoß Gazette

A disciplined 2–0 win over Yaadballers FC on a chaotic afternoon at home was enough to seal it, with a makeshift XI — missing both first-choice goalkeeper The Joker and winger Pacey Lacey through injury, and El Tecla dropped in at center back at fifty-six years old — grinding out the result that had eluded this squad for the better part of a season.

The celebrations that followed will likely outlive the football itself. John Barnes had Dean Syche convinced Santi Cazorla had a shoulder injury, dramatically pointing at the shoulder in question — right up until the changing room speaker dropped the beat and Cazorla launched into a full dance routine instead. By nightfall the entire squad had relocated to a pub near the ground, still in kit, buying rounds for anyone wearing the badge.

Division 1 awaits. Whether this squad is built for it remains to be seen — but for one night, nobody at Galacticoß BPIE cared.
